# Key Insights

- **28M trips** generated approximately **$445.2M in revenue**, with an average fare of **$13.18** per trip.
- **Brooklyn** generated the highest pickup-borough revenue at approximately **$158M**, followed by **Manhattan ($130M)** and **Queens ($124M)**.
- **Saturday** recorded the highest trip activity, while **18:00** was the peak pickup hour.
- **Manhattan → Manhattan** was the largest borough-level trip flow, indicating strong intra-borough demand.
- **Fare revenue contributed approximately 84%** of total revenue, making fares the primary revenue component.
- **Credit cards accounted for 52.2% of trips** and generated higher revenue per trip than cash payments.
- Average trips covered **3.87 miles** and lasted approximately **21.1 minutes**, with an average speed of around **11 mph**.
- **24.2% of trips were 1 mile or less**, highlighting substantial short-distance taxi demand.
- Revenue declined by approximately **66.5% from 2019 to 2020** in the analyzed data.
- Overall, the dashboard highlights **strong demand concentration in major boroughs, fare-driven revenue, and significant short-distance urban travel activity**.
